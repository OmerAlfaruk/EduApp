## 2024-11-20 - Ensure All IconButtons Have Descriptive, State-Appropriate Tooltips
**Learning:** Icon-only buttons like 'IconButton' in Flutter require a descriptive, state-appropriate 'tooltip' property. The tooltip serves as the primary semantic/accessibility label for screen readers, ensuring that visually impaired users can navigate the application intuitively and that all users receive helpful visual feedback on hover.
**Action:** Always specify a state-appropriate 'tooltip' property when utilizing 'IconButton' widgets or any icon-only buttons in Flutter.
