## 2026-02-18 - Enhancing Accessibility in Flutter via Icon-only IconButton Tooltips
**Learning:** In Flutter, icon-only buttons like `IconButton` without a `tooltip` attribute are silent to screen readers (e.g., TalkBack, VoiceOver), creating a significant accessibility gap. By defining custom, state-aware, and context-appropriate `tooltip` properties, we provide standard accessibility descriptions and standard hover/long-press feedback that significantly improve usability and screen reader support without changing the visual layout.
**Action:** Always specify descriptive, state-appropriate `tooltip` attributes for every icon-only `IconButton` or interactive element in Flutter apps, and ensure they dynamically reflect status changes (such as show/hide passwords or editing profile pictures).
