## 2025-02-21 - Accessible Tooltips for Icon-Only Buttons in Flutter
**Learning:** In Flutter, `IconButton` widgets without a `tooltip` lack accessible labels for screen readers, making icon-only navigation controls opaque to visually impaired users.
**Action:** Always provide a concise, descriptive `tooltip` attribute on all icon-only `IconButton` widgets to ensure built-in screen reader support and helpful hover hints.
