## 2024-10-18 - State-appropriate tooltips for Flutter IconButtons
**Learning:** Icon-only `IconButton` widgets in Flutter rely on the `tooltip` property to serve as their primary accessibility label for screen readers and visual tooltips on desktop/web hover.
**Action:** Always provide descriptive, state-appropriate `tooltip` strings for icon-only `IconButton` widgets (including toggle buttons like show/hide password or photo picker buttons).
