## 2025-02-12 - Icon Button Tooltips for Screen Readers
**Learning:** Icon-only buttons (such as IconButton in Flutter) are highly problematic for accessibility (a11y) because they lack explicit text labels. Providing a descriptive, state-appropriate 'tooltip' is the easiest way to give them an accessibility label that screen readers can announce.
**Action:** Always specify a descriptive and dynamic (state-appropriate) 'tooltip' on icon-only buttons to ensure a pleasant and accessible experience.
